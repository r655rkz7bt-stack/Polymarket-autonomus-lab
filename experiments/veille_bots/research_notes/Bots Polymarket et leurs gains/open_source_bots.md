# Open-source Polymarket trading bots and frameworks (2024-2026): what exists, what they claim, what is proven

Scope note: star/fork counts and "updated_at" dates come from the GitHub search API, queried on 2026-10-16 ([GitHub search "polymarket bot"](https://github.com/search?q=polymarket+bot&type=repositories&s=stars), [GitHub search "polymarket"](https://github.com/search?q=polymarket&type=repositories&s=stars)). "updated_at" on GitHub reflects any repo activity (including stars), not necessarily a code commit. Several news domains (kucoin, theblock, dev.to, cryptonews, bitrue, crypto-economy, pm.wiki, unchainedcrypto) failed DNS resolution from the research sandbox, so those facts come from search-engine snippets only and are flagged as such.

## 1. Which repositories exist and what do they do (catalog by category)

### Takeaway
GitHub returned ~4,000 repos for "polymarket bot" and ~14,000 for "polymarket" on 2026-10-16. Most were created between late 2025 and 2026, and most bot repos are thin or promotional. A small number of serious projects exist: warproxxx/poly-maker for market making, kachence/polymm for sports market making with public P&L, ent0n29/polybot for infrastructure, sterlingcrispin/nothing-ever-happens (a meme "always buy No" bot), and a set of data and backtesting tools. The categories that dominate by count are crypto "Up or Down" 5-min/15-min bots, YES+NO complete-set "arbitrage", Polymarket-Kalshi cross-platform arbitrage, and copy-trading.

### Cited Findings

**Market making**
- warproxxx/poly-maker: 1,512 stars, 492 forks, created 2025-03-31. It is a maker-only, post-only market maker for Polymarket CLOB V2 that focuses on political markets. It estimates fair value from the order book adjusted by trade flow, then quotes a buy on YES and a buy on NO. Inventory skews both quotes, and spreads widen with volatility and "toxicity". It has five regimes (quiet, trending, event, reduce-only, halted) and targets Polymarket liquidity rewards plus the maker rebate. Risk controls are exposure caps, a daily-loss kill switch and a stale-data halt. Paper mode is available. Not yet built: a backtester, external feeds, and Safe/proxy merging. — [warproxxx/poly-maker README](https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker)
- One aggregator reports a last push on 2026-04-06. A third-party write-up describes the economics as liquidity rewards, meaning a fixed daily pool split by your share, plus rebates, with diminishing returns once you hold a large share of the pool. — search snippets citing [gittrend.io](https://gittrend.io/repo/warproxxx/poly-maker) and [tread.fi docs](https://docs.tread.fi/prediction-markets/polymarket/market-making-on-polymarket) (not fetched directly)
- Polymarket/poly-market-maker (official "market maker keeper for the Polymarket CLOB"): 327 stars, created 2022-02. — [GitHub](https://github.com/Polymarket/poly-market-maker)
- Other MM repos: terrytrl100/polymarket-automated-mm (37 stars, 2025-12); lihanyu81/polymarket_lp_tool, an LP-rewards tool with 580 stars created 2026-04 and no description; 0xbrutusdev/polymarket-5min-crypto-trading-bot (59 stars, 2026-08), which "mints complete sets, rests post-only asks, merges pairs, and redeems the winner" on BTC 5-min markets. — [GitHub search](https://github.com/search?q=polymarket+bot&type=repositories&s=stars)
- kachence/polymm: 113 stars, created 2026-07-19. A sports market maker and arbitrage bot. It de-vigs sportsbook odds (the-odds-api) to get a fair probability and quotes just above the best bid when the edge is at least `min_edge` (5% by default). When one side fills, it buys the opposite outcome to lock in profit. Leftover one-sided fills become directional bets. — [kachence/polymm README](https://github.com/kachence/polymm)

### Inferences
- Popularity does not track evidence. The most-starred bot repos (CloddsBot, the BTC 15-min bot, the weather bot) publish no verifiable live P&L. The one repo with an on-chain-checkable result (polymm, about $5k) has only 113 stars.
- The crowded categories (BTC 5/15-min, YES+NO<1 arbitrage, Polymarket-Kalshi arbitrage, copy-trading) are the easiest to code and market. That suggests the simple edges are heavily competed. The January 2026 taker fee was aimed directly at the latency edge.
- Data and backtest tooling (poly_data, prediction-market-analysis, Nautilus extension) is arguably more valuable to a simulation lab than most bot repos.

### Gaps
- No dedicated, reputable open-source "0.99 endgame sniper" or "new-market sniper" repo was identified. The category exists mostly inside multi-strategy or "terminal" repos.
- Exact last-commit dates per repo were not retrieved. GitHub "updated_at" includes stars, so it overstates activity.
- I could not fetch Reddit (r/Polymarket, r/algotrading) or Hacker News threads. Search returned only news-aggregator content.

## 2. Official tooling and what it reveals about how bots are meant to operate

### Takeaway
Polymarket's official stack is a hybrid off-chain CLOB with on-chain settlement. It is accessed through CLOB clients, the Gamma metadata API, websockets and a subgraph, and authenticated with a wallet private key. In 2026 the legacy clients (py-clob-client, TS clob-client, rs-clob-client) and the agents repo were archived in favour of a "unified SDK" (Polymarket/py-sdk), a Rust CLI and "agent skills". Polymarket also adjusts fees and rebates to favour makers over latency takers.

### Cited Findings
- Polymarket/py-clob-client (1,227 stars) was **archived 2026-05-25**. Its README says: "The client is no longer functional and should not be used for new or existing integrations. Please migrate to our new unified SDK: https://github.com/Polymarket/py-sdk". — [py-clob-client](https://github.com/Polymarket/py-clob-client)
- The TS Polymarket/clob-client (515 stars) and Polymarket/rs-clob-client (682 stars, created 2025-12) are also archived. Polymarket/ctf-exchange (Solidity) is archived. Newer official repos are Polymarket/polymarket-cli (2,880 stars, Rust, created 2026-02-24), Polymarket/agent-skills (192 stars, 2026-02) and Polymarket/polymarket-subgraph (220 stars). — [GitHub search](https://github.com/search?q=polymarket&type=repositories&s=stars)
- Polymarket/agents was archived 2026-05-11. Its architecture is Gamma API, then order execution, then RAG/Chroma, then the LLM, which shows the intended "AI agent" pattern: retrieve news, estimate, then place orders through the CLOB. — [Polymarket/agents](https://github.com/Polymarket/agents)
- Both poly-maker and nothing-ever-happens need the signer's raw private key (`PK`) in `.env`, plus a funder or proxy address. This is the standard auth pattern, and it is also what malware targets (see section 4). — [poly-maker](https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker); [nothing-ever-happens](https://github.com/sterlingcrispin/nothing-ever-happens)
- Liquidity rewards plus maker rebates are an explicit design target for poly-maker. The taker fee on 15-min crypto markets (January 2026) funds daily USDC rebates to makers. — [poly-maker](https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker); search snippet [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/384461/polymarket-adds-taker-fees-to-15-minute-crypto-markets-to-fund-liquidity-rebates)

### Inferences
- The protocol design (post-only support, rewards and rebates paid to makers, fees charged to takers on short crypto markets) pushes bots toward passive liquidity provision and away from latency taking.
- Any lab still using py-clob-client for live calls should expect it to stop working. For read-only market data, Gamma/CLOB REST endpoints and websockets remain the relevant feeds (endpoint stability not verified here).

### Gaps
- I did not fetch Polymarket/py-sdk or docs.polymarket.com, so the exact current fee schedule, CLOB V2 changes and the real-time data feed specification were not verified first-hand.

## 3. What authors report about results (P&L, warnings, "no longer profitable")

### Takeaway
Credible authors are uniformly cautious. Of the repos reviewed, only polymm gives a concrete live P&L that can be checked on-chain: about +$5k net over a few months in early 2026, before it "mostly stopped being profitable" because it got too slow. Everything else is either unquantified, simulation-only, or a single unverified win-rate figure. Academic work confirms that arbitrage profits were large historically (~$40M, April 2024 to April 2025) but concentrated.

### Cited Findings
- **polymm (kachence)** "finished up about $5k net" over a few months in early 2026. The arb leg made +$8,293 and forced directional residuals lost -$3,184, which the author attributes to adverse selection. The bot "mostly stopped being profitable" and "got too slow to defend its edge". The odds scrapers, a large part of the edge, are not included, and the live version is now a private Rust rewrite. The wallet is public. — [kachence/polymm README](https://github.com/kachence/polymm)
- **poly-maker (warproxxx)**: the current README says "Market making on Polymarket is competitive and can lose money." and "This is a reference implementation and a research harness, not a guaranteed-profitable product." A search for an explicit author statement that the bot is "no longer profitable" found nothing in the current README. That claim, made by the caller, could not be verified here and may come from an earlier README version or from X/Twitter. — [poly-maker README](https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker); [search](https://github.com/warproxxx/poly-maker)
- **nothing-ever-happens**: no P&L, return or backtest figures. "FOR ENTERTAINMENT ONLY." — [README](https://github.com/sterlingcrispin/nothing-ever-happens)
- **Weather bot (suislanchez)**: the About blurb says "(Highest profits $1.8k)" but the README never explains it. The disclaimer calls it "a simulation tool" that "does not place real trades or use real money", while other text references live Kalshi RSA auth. These are inconsistent. — [README](https://github.com/suislanchez/polymarket-kalshi-weather-bot)
- **BTC 15-min bot (aulekator)**: the only figure is "~75% win rate in early runs" in simulation, with no sample size, fees or P&L. The default max is $1 per trade. — [README](https://github.com/aulekator/Polymarket-BTC-15-Minute-Trading-Bot)
- **polybot (ent0n29)**: no P&L. "educational and research purposes only". — [README](https://github.com/ent0n29/polybot)
- **Moon Dev examples**: "none are plug and play make a million dollars over night". — [GitHub](https://github.com/moondevonyt/Polymarket-Trading-Bot-Examples-By-Moon-Dev)
- **Academic**: Saguillo, Ghafouri, Kiffer and Suarez-Tangil (AFT 2025, arXiv 2508.03474) estimate ~$40M realized arbitrage profit on Polymarket from markets resolved April 2024 to April 2025. They split it into intra-market "rebalancing" (sum of outcomes ≠ 1) and cross-market combinatorial arbitrage. — [arXiv 2508.03474](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.03474)
- **Concentration**: a late-2025 on-chain analysis of ~1.7M addresses found ~70% net losers and under 0.04% of addresses capturing over 70% of profits. — search snippet [coin360](https://coin360.com/news/polymarket-traders-losses-profit-concentration-2026-predictions) (secondary; unverified)
- One press figure says an arbitrage bot made "$2.2 million in profit in just two months". — search snippet (MEXC news aggregation) ([mexc.com/news](https://www.mexc.com/news/503593)) (unverified press figure)
- A developer reports that pure latency strategies "stopped working the same day" the 15-min taker fee arrived, because at thin margins the fee flips trades negative. — search snippet [dev.to lkto1m](https://dev.to/lkto1m/february-2026-changed-polymarket-forever-heres-what-happened-to-my-bots-numbers-2fi5) (not fetched)
- A post titled "We lost $270 building a Polymarket trading bot" exists, but its content could not be fetched. — [pm.wiki](https://pm.wiki/news/wire/agg_cbfb988c71e29a64)

### Inferences
- Realistic expectations for an open-source strategy run by a small actor: small absolute gains (thousands of dollars) that decay within months as speed or data edges erode. Adverse selection on one-sided fills is the recurring loss mechanism for maker and arb hybrids.
- The edge lives in non-published parts: fresh data scrapers, speed (Rust rewrites), and private wallets. Public repos ship the "shell".
- The lab's simulation can test the same ideas honestly: complete-set arbitrage net of fees, de-vigged sportsbook quotes, weather ensembles, "buy No" baselines. It should also model adverse selection on resting orders.

### Gaps
- No independently audited P&L exists for any open-source bot. polymm's figure is self-reported but on-chain checkable. I did not look up the wallet.
- I could not retrieve issue threads with user-reported P&L for poly-maker or the BTC bots.

## 4. Scams, malware and warning signs

### Takeaway
Yes, a significant share of the "Polymarket bot" ecosystem is hostile or junk. At least two documented malware campaigns (December 2025 and later) used fake Polymarket copy-trading or arbitrage bots on GitHub to steal private keys from `.env` files through booby-trapped npm dependencies. There is also widespread SEO spam (keyword-stuffed descriptions, stars without forks, accounts years older than the repo topic), affiliate and referral promotion, and at least one likely typosquat SDK.

### Cited Findings
- **December 2025 campaign**: a GitHub repo "polymarket-copy-trading-bot" under the handle "Trust412" carried a hidden payload. On startup it read the wallet key from `.env` and exfiltrated it via the dependency `excluder-mcp-package@1.0.4`. SlowMist and others issued warnings, and the author reportedly altered commits to evade detection. — search snippets from [KuCoin news](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/polymarket-copy-trading-bot-project-found-to-contain-malicious-code-stealing-private-keys), [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605119246), [Bitrue](https://www.bitrue.com/blog/polymarket-bot-github-malware), [cryptonews.net](https://cryptonews.net/news/security/32170648/) (pages not fetchable from sandbox; details from search summaries)
- **Second campaign**: a repo posing as an arbitrage tool was tied to 30 malicious npm packages, with at least 53 developers reportedly infected. The malicious code was hidden in a dependency named `clob-client-math`. — search snippet of [Crypto Economy](https://crypto-economy.com/fake-polymarket-trading-bot-infects-defi-developers-with-credential-stealing-malware/) (not fetched; date not confirmed)
- Polymarket itself was not hacked in either case. Losses came from unofficial bots that require the private key. — search summary of the above sources

### Inferences
- Warning-sign checklist:
  1. The bot needs your private key in `.env` and pulls obscure npm or PyPI dependencies whose names sound like official packages (`clob-client-math`, `@polymarkets/...`).
  2. Many stars but almost no forks or issues, or star bursts on new repos.
  3. Keyword-stuffed descriptions.
  4. An account or repo created long before the topic, which suggests it was bought or repurposed.
  5. "Risk-free" or "guaranteed" claims, or win rates without P&L.
  6. Telegram contact, referral links, or paid "custom bot" offers.
  7. Prebuilt or minified `dist/` files that do not match the source.
  8. Advertised features (live execution, copy trading) absent from the code.
  9. The repo name says "arbitrage" while the code is directional.
- For this lab (no keys, no real orders), the risk is limited to supply-chain risk if any such code were ever imported. The policy of never running downloaded code and pinning official dependencies only is justified.
